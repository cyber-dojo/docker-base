SNYK-GOLANG-GOOGLEGOLANGORGGRPCINTERNALTRANSPORT-18172578 | google.golang.org/grpc/internal/transport | CVSS 8.8 | High | no CVE assigned
What: Incorrect authorization in the processing of xDS RBAC policies, together
with HTTP/2 transport handling. Unsupported fields in policy matchers are
mishandled, letting an attacker bypass authorization checks, trigger a denial of
service through the rapid-reset mitigations, or panic a server with a crafted
policy update. Snyk records the fix as 1.82.1.
For cyber-dojo: the vulnerable copies are in containerd, containerd-shim-runc-v2
and ctr, all vendoring v1.80.0. dockerd, docker-compose and docker-buildx vendor
v1.82.1 and are patched. All three affected binaries serve or consume gRPC over
local Unix sockets rather than a network listener, and cyber-dojo runs no xDS
control plane, so no RBAC policy is evaluated at all. User code runs with
--net=none and so cannot open a connection to any of them, let alone supply a
policy update or an HTTP/2 stream.
Verdict: Not exploitable. The RBAC path is never exercised, and the transport is
not reachable from user code.
